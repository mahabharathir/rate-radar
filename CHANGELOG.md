# Changelog

All notable changes to the Rate Radar project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-01

### Added
- Complete static multi-page architecture designed for GitHub Pages deployment.
- Central Bank Rate Radar: Policy Repo Rate, SDF, MSF, Bank Rate, CRR, and SLR.
- Home loan comparison table across 8 leading Indian public and private banks.
- Term deposit and savings boards: Fixed Deposits (FD), Recurring Deposits (RD), and Government Savings Schemes (PPF, SSA, SCSS, NSC, KVP, MIS).
- Precious Metals Radar: 24K, 22K, 18K gold and silver 999 fine bullion and retail benchmarks.
- Market Radar: Nifty 50, BSE Sensex, Bank Nifty, equities price directory, top gainers, top losers, and sectoral distributions.
- Currency exchange rates and client-side instant currency converter.
- Energy and fuel tracker: Brent & WTI crude benchmarks, city-wise retail petrol and diesel rates, and domestic/commercial LPG cylinder prices.
- Credit section: Comprehensive CIBIL score guide with direct links to official TransUnion CIBIL site, and client-side loan eligibility & EMI calculator.
- Financial calendar: Stock exchange trading & clearing holidays, state-wise bank holiday schedules, and RBI Monetary Policy Committee meeting dates.
- Client-side global instant search with keyboard shortcuts (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`).
- Client-side stale data detection indicator.
- GitHub Actions automated validation and deployment workflow.
